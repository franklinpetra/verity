    Your OpenRouter key

    Verity runs on your own OpenRouter key, so the AI costs go on your OpenRouter account and your documents go only from this browser to the models.

    Tip: give the key a spending limit in OpenRouter. A typical decision costs a few dollars.

    How it works

    1. Independent answers. Each advisor reads your documents and answers alone. None is told the others exist, because knowing there's a room makes models posture, defer, or leave gaps for someone else.
    2. Quote check. Every quote an advisor attributes to your documents is looked up in the text itself. Quotes that aren't there are flagged and shown to the whole board.
    3. Debate. Each advisor reads all the answers and must say where the others are wrong, with evidence. Changing position without a reason gets called out.
    4. Decision brief. One advisor writes the brief: the recommendation and how many back it, where the board agrees, where it splits and why, the risks, and what to find out before deciding. Disagreement is shown, not averaged away.

    A failure never reads as a success: if an advisor times out or its answer is cut off, the run says so and carries on with the rest. Long documents are read whole when they fit; when they don't, an advisor gets the sections most relevant to the decision, and the brief says so.

    Where your data goes

    • Your documents are read in this browser tab. They're never uploaded to us; there is no server behind this page.
    • The models receive your decision and documents through OpenRouter, using your key, and only from providers that don't keep or train on what they're sent.
    • Web search, when you turn it on, sends search queries to OpenRouter's search provider. Leave it off for confidential matters.
    • Your key stays in this browser, for this session only unless you choose to remember it.
    • Nothing else. No analytics, no cookies, no fonts or scripts from other sites. The page's security policy blocks every connection except OpenRouter, so this isn't just a promise.
